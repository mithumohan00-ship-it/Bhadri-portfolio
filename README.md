A clean, modern, responsive portfolio for **Veerabhadri (Bhadri)**, Founder of **Bhadri's Academy**, Academic Mentor, and Student Counselor.

---

## 🌟 Structure & Pages

1. **Home Page ([`index.html`](file:///Users/mithumohan/Desktop/Bhadri/index.html))**:
   - Clean, lightweight, unbloated layout.
   - **Hero Section**: Mentor positioning, portrait, direct contact & WhatsApp buttons, and a "View Gallery →" link.
   - **About Me**: Student-centric mentorship philosophy, 8+ years of guidance, key stats.
   - **What I Do**: Academy Leadership, 1-on-1 Student Mentorship, Mindset & Exam Counseling, Seminars & Workshops.
   - **Contact Section**: Phone & WhatsApp, Email, Location, and quick message form.
   - *Note*: The gallery is NOT displayed on the home page, keeping it fast, focused, and clean.

2. **Dedicated Gallery Page ([`gallery.html`](file:///Users/mithumohan/Desktop/Bhadri/gallery.html))**:
   - Clicking **"Gallery"** in the navbar or hero redirects here.
   - Displays all **19 photoshoot images** (`IMG_7960.jpg` – `IMG_8174.jpg`).
   - **Natural Aspect Ratios**: Responsive masonry layout preserves true 2:3 portrait and 3:2 landscape proportions without any cropping.
   - **Zero Captions**: Pure photo presentation without text clutter.
   - **Full-Screen Lightbox**: Click any image to view in full resolution with Next/Previous navigation and keyboard controls (`Esc`, `←`, `→`).
   - "← Back to Home" button for seamless navigation.
